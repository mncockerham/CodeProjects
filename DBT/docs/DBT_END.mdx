# Stop DBT & Dagster Environment

Follow these steps to completely shut down your data orchestration environment and free up Mac resources when doing other activities.

## 1. Stop Dagster Server
- Go to your Mac terminal window where `dagster dev` is currently running.
- Press `Ctrl + C` on your keyboard to terminate the Dagster webserver.
- Wait a few seconds for the background processes (AssetDaemon, Scheduler, etc.) to gracefully shut down.

## 2. Stop PostgreSQL App
- Click on the **elephant icon** in your Mac's top menu bar (from Postgres.app).
- Click **Stop** to shut down the database server and free up CPU/Memory.
- (Alternatively, you can click "Quit" if you don't even want the elephant icon sitting on your menu bar).

## 3. Close the Terminal Window
- The Dagster python process uses a bit of memory. When you are totally finished with your DBT development session, simply closing the active terminal sessions guarantees no rogue processes are left hanging.
